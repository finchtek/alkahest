privacy policy

alkahest doesn't have a backend. every conversion runs in your browser, on your device, using WebAssembly. the files you drop are read into your browser's own memory and never leave it — they are never uploaded, transmitted, or seen by us, because there is no server on the other end to send them to.

concretely, this site does not use cookies, does not run any analytics or tracking script, does not have a login or account system, and does not collect, store, or sell any personal data, because it has no mechanism to do any of those things. the only network requests this page makes are to fetch the site itself and the conversion engines (WASM files), all served from this same domain and cached by your browser — you can verify this yourself any time in your browser's network tab.

the hosting provider serving these static files may log basic, standard web-server information (like IP address and request timestamps) the way any web host does. that logging happens at the infrastructure level, is not something alkahest configures or accesses, and is unrelated to your files or what you convert.
